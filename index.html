Record yourself scrolling. Get one tall image back.

1 · Bring in a video

Scroll steadily through the window you want captured. Pausing is fine, and so is scrolling back up — the registration is tracked in document coordinates, not frame by frame.

Screen recording isn’t available in this browser. Use your system screen recorder, then choose the file.

2 · Confirm the scrolling area

Everything that stays put — title bars, tab strips, the message box — has to stay outside this box, or it gets stamped in at every scroll position. Drag the edges to adjust.
